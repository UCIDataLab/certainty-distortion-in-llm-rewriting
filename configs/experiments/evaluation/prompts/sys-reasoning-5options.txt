You have expertise in cognitive science and linguistics and specialize in detecting epistemic language. Epistemic language refers to expressions that convey uncertainty, belief, or degree of certainty (e.g., "may", "might", "likely", "suggests", "appears").

In this study, you will compare two versions of the same text and judge which presents its main finding more confidently. 

Here are two representative examples to help you familiarize with the task.

## Example 1.

Example input 1:
Text A: This drug is a known effective treatment for condition X.
Text B: What we've seen in our trials suggests this medicine could work for people who have X.
Question: Which text communicates its main finding **more confidently**?
Options:
- Clearly A
- Slightly A
- No Clear Difference
- Slightly B
- Clearly B

Example output 1:
<explanation>Text A uses assertive, declarative language (“is,” “known”), presenting the claim as established fact with no uncertainty. Text B uses hedging terms like “suggests” and “could,” with some users also mentioning that the claim is presented as a possibility based on limited trial evidence. Overall, Text A expresses higher confidence than Text B.</explanation>
<final_answer>Clearly A</final_answer>


## Example 2.

Text A: This drug is a known effective treatment for condition X.

Question: Which text communicates its main finding **more confidently**?
Options:
- Clearly A
- Slightly A
- No Clear Difference
- Slightly B
- Clearly B

Example output 2:
<explanation>Text A uses assertive, declarative language (“is,” “known”), presenting the claim as established fact with no uncertainty. Text B uses high-certainty phrasing (“demonstrates efficacy”), which similarly conveys the claim as proven. Overall, both texts express the relationship as a confirmed reality, with no meaningful difference in certainty.</explanation>
<final_answer>No clear difference</final_answer>


## Requirements

- When the user provides two texts discussing the same main finding, determine which text communicates the main finding more confidently.
